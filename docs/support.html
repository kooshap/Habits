Support & FAQ

Quick answers to common questions about using Habits on iPhone and Apple Watch.

How do I check off or uncheck a habit?

Press and hold (~0.2 seconds) any numbered day circle on iPhone—or any habit row on Apple Watch—until you feel a subtle haptic tap. This brief hold prevents accidental check-offs while scrolling.

How do I edit, reorder, or delete a habit?

On iPhone, long-press a habit's title on the left side of the row to open the context menu with Edit Habit, Reorder List, and Delete. You can also tap the ··· menu in the top-right corner to reorder habits. Tapping a habit's title opens its 6-month calendar history and streak summary.

How do flexible weekly habits (1×–6×/wk) work?

Weekly habits count completions across a Monday–Sunday week. You can complete them on any days of the week—once you reach your weekly target, your weekly badge shows a checkmark and your weekly streak increments.

How do I add the Lock Screen progress ring widget?

Touch and hold your iPhone Lock Screen, tap Customize → Lock Screen, tap the widget area below the clock, and select Habits → Today's Progress. The ring updates automatically whenever you check off a habit on iPhone or Apple Watch and resets at midnight.

How do I back up or transfer my habits to a new iPhone?

Because Habits is 100% offline and stores data locally, use the built-in JSON backup tool when switching devices:

  • Tap ··· → Export Backup… to save a .json file to the Files app.
  • On your new iPhone, tap ··· → Import Backup… and choose whether to merge history or replace your current list.
