Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

The Short Version

Habits collects no data whatsoever. The app operates completely offline, requires no account or registration, contains no analytics or tracking SDKs, and never transmits any information off your devices.

1. Zero Data Collection

We do not collect, access, store, transmit, rent, or sell any personal information, habit entries, usage metrics, crash reports, device identifiers, location data, or diagnostics. Habits makes zero network requests.

2. No Accounts or Registration

Habits does not use user accounts, sign-ins, email addresses, or cloud databases. You can use every feature of the app immediately without providing any personal details.

3. Local On-Device Storage & Sync

  • iPhone: All habit names, schedules, colors, and completion dates are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework.
  • Lock Screen Widget: A minimal progress summary is shared locally on your iPhone inside a private iOS App Group container solely to render the Lock Screen progress ring widget.
  • Apple Watch: If you use a paired Apple Watch, habit snapshots and check-offs sync directly between your iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple's local WatchConnectivity framework.

4. Manual JSON Backups

Habits includes an optional Export and Import feature that lets you save or restore a plain .json backup file using the standard iOS Files picker. Exported files are written solely to the location you choose (such as On My iPhone or your personal cloud drive). The developer never receives or has access to your backup files.

5. Third-Party Services

Habits includes zero third-party frameworks, advertising SDKs, or analytics trackers. Even this webpage uses zero cookies, scripts, or analytics.

6. Deleting Your Data

Because all data lives locally on your devices, you have complete control over it at all times. You can delete individual habits inside the app, or permanently erase all stored data by uninstalling Habits from your iPhone and Apple Watch.

7. Changes & Contact

If this policy is ever updated, the revised version and effective date will be posted on this page. If you have any questions about privacy or how Habits works, please reach out via our Support page.
